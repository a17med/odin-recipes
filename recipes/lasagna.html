Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, rich meat sauce, creamy cheese, and tomato sauce.

It is baked in the oven until the cheese melts and the top becomes golden and delicious.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Olive oil

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  3. Cook the ground beef with chopped onion and garlic.
  4. Add tomato sauce and season with salt and pepper.
  5. Spread a layer of meat sauce in a baking dish.
  6. Add noodles, ricotta, mozzarella, and more meat sauce.
  7. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used.
  8. Top with mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  9. Bake for 45 minutes until golden and bubbling.
  10. Let it rest for 10 minutes before serving.
