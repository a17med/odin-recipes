Pizza

Description

Pizza is a popular Italian dish made with a round dough base, tomato sauce, and melted cheese.

It can be topped with vegetables, meat, or other ingredients and baked until the crust becomes golden and crispy.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Olive oil
  • Fresh basil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough into a round shape.
  3. Spread tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  4. Add mozzarella cheese on top.
  5. Drizzle with a little olive oil.
  6. Bake for 10–15 minutes until the crust is golden.
  7. Add fresh basil before serving.
