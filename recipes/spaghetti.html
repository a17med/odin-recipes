Spaghetti

Description

Spaghetti is a traditional Italian pasta dish often served with a rich tomato sauce and ground beef.

It is easy to prepare and makes a delicious meal for lunch or dinner.

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti pasta
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • One onion
  • Two cloves of garlic
  • Olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
  2. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions.
  3. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat.
  4. Add chopped onion and garlic and cook until soft.
  5. Add ground beef and cook until browned.
  6. Pour in the tomato sauce and season with salt and pepper.
  7. Let the sauce simmer for 10–15 minutes.
  8. Drain the spaghetti and serve with the sauce.
  9. Top with Parmesan cheese before serving.
